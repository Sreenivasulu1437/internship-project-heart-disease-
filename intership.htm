Laza Medical, a privately held portfolio company of Shifamed, has announced the successful completion of its first software-only human cases using its AI-powered ultrasound image guidance technology. Conducted at Oregon Health Sciences University Hospital, the evaluation marked the first clinical use of Laza’s real-time, patient-specific 3D heart model alongside live procedures.

The software operated concurrently during cases to enable retrospective analysis, marking an early step toward future testing of Laza’s complete robotic imaging system. Designed to reduce the complexity and resource demands of structural heart interventions, the platform builds 3D models from ultrasound data alone, aiming to provide consistent anatomical context without relying on manual adjustments.

Clinical experts welcomed the advancement as a step forward in procedural imaging. Dr. Scott Chadderdon, Head of Cardiac Imaging at Oregon Health Sciences University Hospital, stated, “There is a significant need for solutions that can help make high-quality imaging guidance widely accessible for structural heart procedures.” Dr. Firas Zahr, Head of Interventional Cardiology at the same institution, added, “This study is an important first step in assessing this capability. Once this has been validated, layering in robotic control could unlock a whole new level of efficiency and autonomy.” 

Laza’s ultimate goal is to pair its AI-generated models with robotic control of transesophageal echocardiography to expand access and ease the clinical burden of cardiac imaging.
